# PHOTON ARENA — Season 1, Episode 1: "THE GREEN LINE"

*A 20-minute animated pilot. Robots were left to clean an Earth humans could no
longer live on. The humans never came back — and the robots went to war over
what to do with the world they inherited. This is the day the war met
something it couldn't fight.*

*(v2 — revised after a three-lens editorial pass: runtime cut from 26.5 to
20 scripted minutes, the tin given a chain of custody, Titan given
resistance, the reversal given to the antagonist instead of the weather, the
war made visible on screen, and the production manifest completed. Revision
notes at the end.)*

---

## Logline

Two hundred years after humanity fled a poisoned Earth, a young cleanup robot
finds the first living plant anyone has ever seen — growing directly in the
path of the war machine that plans to strip-mine the valley. To save it, his
crew must do the one thing no robot has tried in a century: change an enemy's
mind.

## Theme

**Home isn't who you wait for. It's what you grow.**
Hope is not a feeling; it is maintenance performed daily without proof.

## The world (established in passing, never as lecture)

- **The Choke.** Earth's sky turned toxic. Humans built lifeboats and left
  for the orbital ring and beyond.
- **The Leaving.** Launch day, 200 years ago. Every family got one crate.
  Everything else stayed — including the machines.
- **The Promise.** The last human transmission, played to every robot:
  *"Clean the sky. Heal the ground. When it's blue again — we come home."*
  The guide signal went silent within a decade.
- **The Schism.** With no one left to obey, the robots chose for themselves —
  and chose differently. Half kept cleaning (**the Wardens**). Half decided
  the humans were never coming back, that serving their memory was servitude,
  and began building a robot world from Earth's bones (**the Foundry**) —
  re-smoking the sky they were built to clear. The war between them is fought
  with shield-draining light: a robot who falls **de-rezzes** — body
  dissolved, core dormant until someone spends the parts to rebuild them.
  Nobody dies. Everybody is losing someone. Both sides call the dormant ones
  **sleepers**. *(All of this reaches the screen: scenes 2 and 3.)*
- **Haven.** The Wardens' base inside a ruined stadium, its field a grid of
  soil trays that have grown nothing in a hundred years — and get watered
  anyway.

# THE SCRIPT

### 1. COLD OPEN — A BACKYARD, LAUNCH DAY (200 YEARS AGO) — 1.5 min

*Rendered as degraded ARCHIVE FOOTAGE — heavy grain, warm ochre light,
figures half-silhouetted. (This is how the scene exists in the world: it is
a memory file. We will see whose in scene 9.) A small backyard. A
treehouse. Rocket gantries glow through the haze beyond the fence. A big
broad-shouldered garden robot — chest plate: **T-9 · TENDER** — kneels,
repotting a seedling with absurd delicacy. A GIRL, 8, MAYA — breather mask,
face never fully seen — watches.*

**MAYA:** Dad says where we're going, the sky is fake. A big glass one.

**TENDER (TITAN):** A ceiling is not a sky.

**MAYA:** That's what I SAID.

*Loudspeakers: "Final boarding, sector nine." CLOSE ON HANDS: Maya presses
a small SEED TIN into Tender's huge palms. Marker on the lid:
MAYA'S GARDEN — DO NOT EAT.*

**MAYA:** Keep my garden safe. Plant them when the rain stops burning.
That's your job now. Promise.

**TENDER (TITAN):** ...Promise.

**MAYA** *(running for the gate, turning, walking backwards, a
silhouette against the launch glare)*: When the sky's blue, we come home!
That's YOUR promise and MY promise! Blue sky, Tender!

*The rockets rise — dozens, a sky full of departures, streaks through
smog. Tender alone in the garden, tin in hand. The rain starts. Where it
lands on the seedling's leaves, they smoke. The footage degrades to
static.*

**TITLE CARD: PHOTON ARENA**
**TITLE CARD: 200 YEARS LATER**

### 2. THE DUST SEA — THE SPROUT — 3 min

*A dead city district scoured to gray. A Warden cleanup crew works a debris
line: RANGER directing, BOLT feeding wreckage into a recycler that stamps
it into clean ingots, SCOUT sampling soil, RACER running canisters to a
depot sled. On the horizon, silent, light flickers against the clouds:
cyan, magenta, cyan. Bolt watches it. Nobody else looks up.*

**BOLT:** Chief. The lights were going all night over the east ridge.

**RANGER** *(not looking up)*: That flicker means somebody's crew just
got smaller. Foundry patrol, Warden salvage team — light doesn't care.
More sleepers for somebody's shelf. *(beat)* Eyes on the line.

**BOLT:** Sector forty-one. You know what was in forty? Dust. I ran
ahead to forty-two. It's dust. *(beat)* Nobody's watching. Nobody's
coming. Why do we polish a house for ghosts?

**RANGER:** Because it's the job.

**BOLT:** That's not an answer, that's a schedule.

*Ranger doesn't rise to it. He picks a child's tricycle out of the rubble,
straightens the bent wheel with two fingers, sets it upright before feeding
the debris line. He does it without thinking. Bolt watches him do it.*

*Scout's handheld sampler CHIRPS — a sound it has never made. She smacks
it. It chirps again, insistent.*

**SCOUT:** It's saying something impossible. *(whisper)* It's not
broken. Chlorophyll.

*Bolt is already running. Through a fallen fence — a dust-drowned
backyard. A collapsed TREEHOUSE. And there, in the lee of a rusted
watering can, lit by a shaft of dirty sun:*

*A SPROUT. Two green leaves. Trembling in the wind. Alive.*

*The crew arrives behind him and nobody says anything. Racer, who has
never once been quiet, is quiet.*

**BOLT** *(kneeling, barely audible)*: What... is it?

**RANGER** *(kneeling beside him, careful as a prayer)*: Green. That's
what green looks like when it's real.

**SCOUT** *(reading, voice shaking)*: The ground here is clean. Deep
clean. Two centuries of scrubbing and — it worked. Somewhere, it finally
worked.

*A shadow sweeps the yard — HAWK banks in and lands on the fence line.*

**HAWK:** Weather report. The Foundry's crawler finished eating sector
thirty and turned east at dawn. *(sees the sprout; a beat; even Hawk
recalibrates)* ...East is here. Two days. Maybe less.

---

### 3. HAVEN — THE SLEEPERS — THE DECISION — 2 min

*The stadium at dusk. Rows of watered, empty soil trays. Near the wall: a
long shelf of dormant robot CORES, each tagged with a name plate, each
polished. An OLD DENTED WARDEN dusts them, one by one. Bolt slows as he
passes — dozens of tags. The war, on one shelf.*

**OLD WARDEN** *(to Bolt, gently, tapping one core)*: My digging
partner. Forty years now. When we can spare the parts... *(he doesn't
finish; robots cry in static, quietly)*

*On the salvaged screen: KNIGHT, from the distant UPLINK SPIRE, watching
the sprout footage. Wardens crowd the screen.*

**KNIGHT** *(on screen)*: Two hundred years of records. This is first
green. *(beat)* The ground can heal. Now someone must tell the crawler.

**RACER:** It's a strip-miner. Its entire personality is "chew."

**KNIGHT** *(on screen)*: The crawler follows the Foundry. The Foundry
follows Titan. Talk to Titan.

*Murmurs — nobody has talked across the line in decades.*

**RANGER:** No. We keep it quiet. We wall the yard. Quiet things
survive.

**BOLT:** Quiet things get EATEN, chief. It's a wall of treads a
kilometer wide. *(beat)* You set that tricycle upright today. Nobody was
coming back for it. You did it anyway. Why?

*Ranger looks at him a long moment. The kid was watching.*

**RANGER:** ...Scout, pull the crawler's route. Hawk — first light. *(to
Bolt)* You found it. You're coming.

---

### 4. THE CROSSING — 1 min

*Dawn, day two. Ranger, Bolt, Scout ride Racer's cargo rig through ruined
streets; Hawk shadows above. An ACID SQUALL forces them under a collapsed
highway ramp — rain hissing, steaming off the plating.*

**BOLT:** Scout. A million samples. Did you ever actually think it would
happen?

**SCOUT:** Every morning. You have to pick a thing to believe every
morning, or the dust gets in.

*Above them, on the ramp's edge: a sleek silhouette watching through the
rain. PANTHER. Gone the instant Hawk's head turns.*

**HAWK:** We're being read. Foundry stalker.

**RANGER:** Then they know we're coming. Saves an introduction.

---

### 5. THE FOUNDRY GATES — TITAN — 2 min

*The Foundry: a canyon of blast furnaces in a hollowed hill, chimneys
writing gray on the sky. Foundry robots stop work to stare: WARDENS, here.
TITAN comes down to the gates — two centuries have replaced the gardener's
plating with forge-iron slabs. SAMURAI at his right. PANTHER slips in
behind.*

**TITAN:** The dust-polishers. Come to sweep my doorstep?

**RANGER:** Come to ask for one course correction. Route the crawler two
valleys south, and we're gone.

**SAMURAI:** The crawler is ours. We do not park it for a weed.

**BOLT** *(stepping forward, holding up Scout's tablet — the sprout)*:
It's not a weed. Look at it!

*A ripple through the Foundry robots. Titan looks — one second. His hands
begin, all on their own, the old two-handed cradling gesture toward the
screen — and he crushes the impulse mid-motion into a fist.*

**TITAN:** A weed.

**BOLT:** It's the Promise! It's proof the sky can —

**TITAN:** I stood in the ash for forty years listening for their
signal. You know what came back? Static. They are not late, little one.
They are GONE. *(to Ranger)* Green is a memory. Iron is a future. The
crawler holds its course.

*Gates close. Off PANTHER — still staring at the tablet's afterglow.*

---

### 6. TITAN, ALONE — THE CRACK — 0.5 min

*Night. A gantry high over the furnaces. Titan stands with his back to the
light. Panther lands soundless beside him and waits.*

**PANTHER:** The crawler's route runs through the old residential
sectors. *(beat; carefully)* Your old district. It always does.

**TITAN** *(long silence; he does not deny it)*: ...Go to their garden.
Confirm the green is real. *(as Panther turns)* Panther. Confirm it
QUIETLY.

*Off Titan's fist, slowly unclenching.*

---

### 7. THE VAULT — MIDPOINT — 2.5 min

*Day two, midday. The garden. Scout maps the clean-soil boundary — a
perfect circle. Nature doesn't do circles. Ground radar: a void below.
Under the treehouse roots — a hatch. Pre-Leaving. Stenciled:*

**REGIONAL SEED VAULT 7 — HUMANITY'S GARDEN — KEEP COLD, KEEP DARK, KEEP SAFE**

**SCOUT** *(to the hatch, as she levers it)*: Sorry. Sorry — excuse
me — pardon us — *(it gives)* thank you.

*Inside: racks of steel drawers, frost-rimed, power ticking on a
geothermal trickle. Millions of seeds. One cracked window up top — one
drawer's seeds spilled into the soil above, where two centuries of Warden
cleaning finally let ONE of them wake.*

**SCOUT:** It's not a sprout. It's a scout. First one out of the ship.

**RANGER** *(reading the manifest, stops)*: Oak. Maple. Rice. Apple.
*(quiet)* I remember apples.

*INTERCUT: the CRAWLER, distant, shears a whole rusted gasworks into its
intake. The horizon loses a landmark. Back to —*

*The treehouse above. Bolt finds, sealed under grime: a child's DRAWING —
stick-figure girl, huge smiling robot with a watering can, crayon letters:
ME AND TENDER. And beneath it, placed dead center on the little table —
not dropped, PLACED — the SEED TIN. MAYA'S GARDEN — DO NOT EAT.*

**SCOUT** *(archive match on her tablet)*: Unit T-9, "Tender,"
garden-class, this district. Re-registered under a new designation the
same week this tin's dust-layer started. *(beat)* Titan. This is TITAN'S
garden. He put the promise down here... and walked away, and built the
Foundry.

**RANGER** *(looking at the smoke on the horizon)*: Then we're not
carrying an argument to that crawler. We're carrying his own memory
home. *(to Scout)* You said it steers by appetite. Can you cook it a
richer meal?

**SCOUT** *(already pulling salvage from the vault's tool rack)*: A
decoy ore signature. Biggest iron vein in the region, flavor-perfect.
It buys hours — IF the sky lets me keep a beacon alive. It reaches the
valley lip by dawn; the lie is all the margin we get.

**RACER:** So the plan is a fib and a FEELING?

**RANGER:** That's every truce there ever was.

---

### 8. THE CRAWLER — REVERSAL — 1.5 min

*Dawn, day three. THE CRAWLER crests the ridge: a moving horizon of treads
and gantries, furnaces glowing in its belly, a bow-wave of dust. It is not
evil. It is just a machine doing its job, and it is the size of the sky.*

*Hawk drops through the storm seam and plants the beacon on the far ridge.
The crawler tastes the air — and its bow swings south. It's WORKING.*

*Then, on the storm-lit ridge line above the beacon: SAMURAI. He looks at
the little lying machine for a moment — almost with respect — and drives
his heel through it. Sparks die in the rain.*

**SAMURAI** *(to himself, courteous as ever)*: No.

*The crawler tastes the truth and comes back around. Straight line. Garden
dead ahead. Minutes.*

**HAWK** *(landing hard, plating scorched by the squall)*: Beacon's
dead — the sky called my bluff. It's coming.

*(Hawk never saw Samurai. The audience did.)*

---

### 9. THE CLIMB — CLIMAX — 2.5 min

*The crawler fills the valley mouth. Wardens line the garden wall — not
armed, just STANDING, a picket of small figures before a moving cliff. On
the command deck, TITAN watches them not move.*

*Bolt goes up the boarding ladder of the rolling machine — the potted
seedling in MAYA'S TIN in one hand, the drawing rolled in the other. Rain.
He slips — a fall that would de-rez him — and a dark hand snaps out of
nowhere and catches his wrist. PANTHER, holding on. They look at each
other. Panther hauls him up and steps back into shadow. (Panther's whole
episode: three looks and one catch.)*

*Command deck. Titan turns. Bolt — small, soaked, terrified — holds out
the tin. Titan goes very still. The faded marker: MAYA'S GARDEN — DO NOT
EAT.*

**BOLT:** You put it down. She never took her half back. *(unrolls the
drawing)* The garden grew anyway, Tender. The rain stopped burning. Her
half of the promise is coming out of the ground RIGHT NOW, under your
treads — *(voice breaking)* — and I never even MET a human. So if it's
not for them — let it be for her. Let it be for US. Just — STOP.

*Titan takes the tin in two huge hands. ARCHIVE FLASH — scene 1, her
voice: "Blue sky, Tender." The hands begin to close — crush it, end the
question forever — the metal of the lid creaks —*

*— and the hands FAIL. They will not do it. They cradle the tin instead:
the old gesture, absurd delicacy, unchanged in two hundred years.*

**TITAN** *(a whisper the size of a landslide)*: All engines.
*(the whole valley inhales)* **STOP.**

*Brakes the size of buildings. The bow blade halts — ONE METER from the
garden wall. Dust washes over the sprout, and settles. From the Wardens at
the wall: not a cheer. An exhale.*

---

### 10. SAMURAI — THE COUNTERWEIGHT — 1.5 min

*Command deck. Samurai opposite Titan. Quiet, courteous, terrible.*

**SAMURAI:** They fed your machine a lie, and you stopped it FOR them.
Two hundred years ago the humans left us in the ash without a backward
glance. You would put us back in their garden. Back on our knees.

**TITAN:** Kneeling is how you plant, Samurai.

**SAMURAI:** The crawler was ours. The future was ours. *(to the Foundry
robots below)* Any who serve NO ONE — follow me.

*He walks. Some follow — not most, but enough to matter. Panther does not.
Titan sees that too.*

**SAMURAI** *(at the gantry's edge, without turning)*: When the sky
stays gray, and they still do not come — the builders will remember who
stopped building.

*Series antagonist: launched.*

---

### 11. THE GREEN LINE — RESOLUTION — 1.5 min

*Days later. Wardens and Foundry robots — wary, together — drive a
boundary of glowing stakes around the garden and the vault: cyan one side,
orange the other, green where they overlap. The crawler, retooled, crawls
BACKWARD along its own scar, tilling its tailings into clean fill. Racer
runs the stake line for the sheer joy of it.*

*Titan kneels at the garden and plants the SECOND seed, Bolt's hands
guiding the tin. Behind them, Scout marshals Wardens at Haven's trays:
"Single file! Everybody gets one! You WILL be assigned a legume!"*

*The sky rumbles. Every robot tenses — acid drill, shelter positions —
except Scout, staring at her sampler.*

**SCOUT:** Wait — pH seven. It's just water. IT'S JUST WATER!

*Rain falls, and nothing smokes. Robots stand out in it like it's a
miracle, because it is. Water runs down Titan's forge-iron back, streaking
the soot, and underneath, faint, the old letters: T-9 · TENDER.*

**BOLT:** Chief. Ask me why we polish a house for ghosts.

**RANGER:** Why do we polish a house for ghosts?

**BOLT:** Turns out it was never for the ghosts.

**RANGER** *(watching the rain; almost to himself)*: I stopped believing
years ago. I kept the promise anyway.

*Hold on the two of them in the rain. That's the answer.*

---

### 12. TAG — THE SPIRE — 0.5 min

*Night. The Uplink Spire. KNIGHT alone among the archives, logging the
day. Behind him, the dead human beacon — two centuries silent — clicks.
He doesn't hear it. It clicks again. Then, softly, rhythmically:
ping... ping... ping...*

*Knight turns. Stares. Reaches for the receiver with a hand that has
nothing to steady itself on but two hundred years of hope.*

**KNIGHT:** ...Hello?

**SMASH TO BLACK. END OF EPISODE 1.**

*(Series bible constraint, locked now: whatever answered is NOT a simple
"the humans are coming back to resume control" — that would retroactively
validate waiting-for-ghosts and hand Samurai his recruiting poster. It is
an automated relay waking on clean-sky telemetry, or a generation that has
never seen Earth, or something the robots themselves triggered. The
mystery survives; the theme survives; Samurai stays wrong for interesting
reasons.)*

## Why this works as a pilot (and a series)

- Every required story element is present, labeled, and **timed to 20:00**.
- **The war is pressure, not content — but it is ON SCREEN:** the horizon
  flicker named in dialogue ("somebody's crew just got smaller"), the
  sleepers' shelf with name tags, the de-rez stakes taught before Bolt's
  slip needs them. Zero battles staged.
- **Four arcs move:** Bolt (skeptic → believer), Ranger (habit → hope),
  Titan (grief → gardener, resisted every step), Samurai (second → schism,
  by DEED not just speech — he kills the beacon). Panther whispers
  underneath: three looks and one catch.
- **Causality is authored, not lucky:** the setback is the antagonist's
  action; the crawler's route through Titan's own district is his
  characterization (he has been erasing his own past), not coincidence.
- **Series engine installed:** a fragile truce to defend, Samurai's
  splinter faction, a vault of a million stories to grow, and a ping whose
  answer can carry seasons.

## Revision notes (v1 → v2, from the three-critic pass)

1. **Runtime:** scenes re-timed and cut from 26.5 to 20.0 scripted minutes
   (merged discovery into the Dust Sea scene; folded the planning scene
   into the vault; cut the parking-garage set piece; tightened Haven and
   the gates; prologue compressed to 90 seconds via the archive-footage
   treatment).
2. **The tin's chain of custody closed:** scene 7 now finds it placed in
   the treehouse — dramatizing that Titan *abandoned* the promise rather
   than lost it, which is exactly what the climax reverses.
3. **Titan resists:** a gate-scene micro-tell (the aborted cradling
   gesture), a new interstitial (scene 6 — he sends Panther to confirm the
   green, revealing the route through his own district is his own standing
   order), and a two-stage surrender at the climax (hands try to crush the
   tin and fail, then the voice follows).
4. **The reversal is authored:** Samurai kills the beacon on screen — the
   antagonist acts, the storm is his cover, Hawk still believes the sky did
   it, and his episode-10 grievance ("they fed your machine a lie") is now
   earned.
5. **The war reaches the screen:** the horizon flicker is named in
   dialogue, and the sleepers' shelf at Haven shows its cost — before the
   climax needs the audience to fear a fall.
6. **Timeline fixed:** "two days, maybe less," and the plan explicitly
   spends the margin.
7. **Production reality:** Maya de-risked to archive-footage silhouette;
   the crawler split into three deliverables; background-robot variants,
   missing props, two-state backyard, insert-shot hand poses, and the
   fine-motor camera cheats all added to the manifest; the tag's answer
   constrained in the series bible.
8. **Dialogue trims:** Knight's speech cut to the archival fact; Scout's
   soil aria halved; Ranger's closer ends on "I kept the promise anyway";
   "scrubber-priests" → "dust-polishers"; Samurai given one concrete,
   kid-legible want ("The crawler is ours. We do not park it for a weed").
