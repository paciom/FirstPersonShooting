### `e_watch` NOT YOUR WAR — ENDING (BAD)

*from: `n29`*

**He holds the line. The line does not save the kid.**

Panther does not move, and the kid falls, and the light of a de-rez goes up in the rain like a small cold firework. The tin bounces off a tread and rolls into the mud. Nobody on the deck ever hears the story that went with it. By noon the yard is gone, the vault under it is gone, and there is a new tag on the shelf at Haven with a young unit's name on it.

*Somewhere on that shelf, dusted daily by an old dented Warden, is the only witness left who can say the green was ever really there.*
