The hero: RANGER, the white robot with the rounded helmet and the glowing cyan visor, cyan-lit panels set across chunky white armor, from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. LIGHTING RULE: one distant pale-orange sun lights everything; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — THE SPAWNFIELDS: a world that grows its own landscape. The plains are packed from horizon to horizon with colossal translucent bladders, some the size of hills and some the size of mountains, each holding a curled, half-formed piece of terrain suspended in glowing sap — a folded ridge, a coiled spire, a rolled-up canyon waiting to be born. When a bladder splits, the terrain inside unfolds, stiffens and becomes real ground, and the older land at the edges of the fields is nothing but the unfolded remains of bladders that hatched long ago. There is no sea and nothing green: only sacs, sap and freshly unfolded rock-flesh.

HATCHING RULE: bladders split and unfold constantly, all over the world, without warning — new ridges rear up out of the plain in seconds, new spires unroll, and the ground map is never the same twice. A bladder swells and goes bright just before it opens. Sap sheets off the new terrain and dries into glassy amber crust.
