A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: RANGER, the white robot with the rounded helmet and the glowing cyan visor, cyan-lit panels set across chunky white armor, from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: one distant pale-gold sun lights everything; consistent light direction and long raking shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — THE MIGRATION: on this planet the continents walk. Each landmass is a single colossal creature-plate, hundreds of kilometres wide, a slab of ridged grey-violet hide carrying its own mesas and dust valleys on its back, striding on stubby buttress limbs across a shallow sea of dry silver mineral powder. They move in a herd, slowly, all in the same direction, raising kilometre-high walls of shining dust behind them. There is nothing green anywhere, no water, no shoreline — only walking land and the powder plain it wades through.

MIGRATION RULE: the landmasses never stop moving. Gaps between neighbouring plates open and close continuously as they walk, so any route across the world is temporary; a canyon that exists in one shot is a closing crush in the next. Every plate rocks with a slow gait, tilting its whole landscape a few degrees each step, and dust cascades off its flanks in constant falls.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. RANGER drifts in orbit above a silver-grey world where the continents are visibly out of place — dark ridged shapes ploughing long curved wakes through a bright powder ocean, all drifting the same way in a slow herd — visibly NOT Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 009 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall RANGER transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first. A sweeping continuous flyover: skimming the silver powder sea through the towering dust wake of a walking plate; pulling up the creature's ridged flank and over its back, racing across mesa country that tilts under the jet as the plate takes a step; threading the narrowing gorge between two plates as their walls grind closer; then climbing to reveal the landmark — THE ELDER, the largest plate of all, its back crowned by a spiral ridge of white bone-stone, leading the whole migration. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): RANGER transforms back into the robot and lands in the closing gap between the Elder and its neighbour, where the golden light is coming from — a chimney of light rising from deep in the crush. It descends fast, boots skidding on the moving hide, dust pouring past. The two plates surge together; a shelf shears away and RANGER falls, catching a single ridge of hide one-handed above the grinding floor. It swings, kicks off the opposite wall, and lands on a lip of rock as the walls scrape shut behind it.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): wedged in a fold of the Elder's hide, held like a splinter the creature could not shake loose, is the artifact from the reference image — a single fist-sized golden metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs, rising on a column of light as RANGER pulls it free. The glyphs flare — and the entire herd STOPS. Every walking continent on the planet halts mid-stride together, the dust walls collapse into slow silver curtains, and one by one the great plates turn their whole landscapes to face the small white robot holding the light.

THE RITUAL CLOSE (28-30s): close-up on RANGER's open palm holding the ONE cube — there is only ever this single cube on screen. Its glyphs pulse and golden holographic text appears in the air beside it reading exactly "FRAGMENT — SECURED" (this exact text, two words only, NO numbers or digits anywhere in it). A calm, epic voice speaks the episode's only words: "Fragment nine... secured." Final frame holds on the robot and the single glowing cube, the halted continents standing silent behind it.
