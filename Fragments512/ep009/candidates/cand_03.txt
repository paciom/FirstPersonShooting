A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: RANGER, the white robot with the rounded helmet and the glowing cyan visor, cyan-lit panels set across chunky white armor, from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: one distant white sun lights everything; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — THE GRAFTS: a planet where the landscape is grown, not built, by a single alien organism that covers it. Every structure is living pale-green chitin: arch-spans a kilometre long, hollow spires, ribbed vaults and buttresses, all smooth-shelled and softly translucent, all still growing at their tips like slow candle wax. Nothing here is stone and nothing here is dead. There is no soil, no water, no vegetation — only shell architecture, layer grown on layer, filling the valleys to the horizon.

HEALING RULE: this world repairs itself, fast and constantly. Any hole punched in the chitin closes within seconds as pale new shell flows in from the edges; any broken span regrows a stub and reaches out to rejoin. The world does not attack — it simply heals, which means every opening the robot makes starts sealing the instant it is made, and the way back is never there any more.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. RANGER drifts in orbit above a planet with no oceans and no continents — only a pale green lacework of grown arches and shell-domes wrapping the entire globe, faintly veined, faintly growing — visibly NOT Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 009 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall RANGER transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first. A sweeping continuous flyover: threading the gaps in a forest of hollow shell spires that lean slowly toward the passing jet; carving under a colossal grown arch-span while new chitin creeps visibly across its underside; punching through a thin membrane wall that seals itself shut behind the exhausts in a heartbeat; then rising to reveal the landmark — THE SCAR, a chasm three kilometres across where something long ago tore the shell open, its rim thick with layered healing growth and slowly, visibly closing shut. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): RANGER transforms back into the robot and rappels into the Scar, dropping past walls that reach toward each other as it descends, new shell flowing over its handholds. It cuts a passage with its blade and squeezes through as the cut knits closed on its ankle; a slab of fresh growth pins its leg and holds it fast in the dark. RANGER braces, cracks the soft new shell with two hammer blows, and drops free onto the chasm floor as the gap above seals over the sunlight.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): at the bottom the organism has grown a smooth pale nodule around the thing it could not absorb, and inside the translucent shell a golden light burns. RANGER splits it open — the artifact from the reference image, a single fist-sized golden metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs, rises on a column of light into its hand. The glyphs flare — and across the whole planet the growth reverses in one breathtaking wave: the Scar unseals and opens wide, arches part, spires bow outward, and a corridor of clean sunlight is grown open from the chasm floor all the way to the sky.

THE RITUAL CLOSE (28-30s): close-up on RANGER's open palm holding the ONE cube — there is only ever this single cube on screen. Its glyphs pulse and golden holographic text appears in the air beside it reading exactly "FRAGMENT — SECURED" (this exact text, two words only, NO numbers or digits anywhere in it). A calm, epic voice speaks the episode's only words: "Fragment nine... secured." Final frame holds on the robot and the single glowing cube, pale shell spires standing open behind it.
