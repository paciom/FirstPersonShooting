A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: RANGER, the white robot with the rounded helmet and the glowing cyan visor, cyan-lit panels set across chunky white armor, from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: one distant violet-white sun lights everything; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — THE FLINCH: the entire surface of this planet is one continuous field of reflex fronds — kelp-like blades of pale lilac tissue, each as tall as a tower, standing shoulder to shoulder from pole to pole. They are all one organism and they all feel the same touch. Between the frond fields lie bare violet salt-flats scoured smooth, and low ridges of chalky root-mass. There is no water, no sky-life, no rock formations — only the standing fronds, swaying together in one direction like a single slow animal.

REFLEX RULE: touch this world anywhere and it flinches. Contact makes the fronds around the point of contact snap flat to the ground in an instant, and the collapse spreads outward as a visible circular wave, racing across the landscape faster than the robot can run. Behind the wave the fronds slowly stand back up. Any disturbance, anywhere, sends a new wave.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. RANGER drifts in orbit above a lilac world with no seas and no clouds, its surface shimmering as huge circular ripples of colour race across whole hemispheres and fade — visibly NOT Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 009 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall RANGER transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first. A sweeping continuous flyover: skimming the frond canopy so the blades slam flat in a widening V behind the jet like a wake; banking down a corridor between two frond walls that clap shut behind the exhausts; racing a ripple wave across a violet salt-flat and outrunning it; then pulling up to reveal the landmark — THE GREAT BUD, a closed cathedral-sized flower-form of overlapping lilac petals standing alone on the flats, sealed tight, with golden light leaking between its seams. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): RANGER transforms back into the robot and lands far out on the flats, then approaches the Bud in absolute stillness — one slow step, wait, one slow step — because every hurried footfall sends a flinch wave that makes the Bud clamp tighter. It reaches the petals and eases two of them apart. A pebble skitters from its heel; the wave races out and rebounds off a distant ridge, and the whole Bud spasms shut on RANGER's arm. Trapped, it stops fighting, holds perfectly still until the petals relax a finger's width, then slips inside.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): in the Bud's warm hollow, cupped in a spiral of soft pale filaments, sits the artifact from the reference image — a single fist-sized golden metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs, rising on a column of light into RANGER's palm. The glyphs flare — and one titanic flinch wave leaves the Bud and crosses the ENTIRE planet: every frond on every continent folds flat in a golden ripple that runs to the horizon and around the curve of the world, laying the whole surface bare, then standing up again all at once.
