# Episode 009 — RANGER — candidate selection

Domain: THE LIVING WORLD (the planet is itself an organism, or wholly colonised
by one alien life-form).

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — RESPIRA, the breathing planet.** The crust is one lung-membrane:
   pale ribbed plains rise and sink on a planet-wide breath and ridge-vents blow
   white spore columns; RANGER races down the throat of the Great Spiracle
   between muscle rings closing on the inhale.
2. **cand_02 — HEMOS, the circulatory planet.** Every canyon is a vessel running
   with amber fluid that surges on a planetary pulse; RANGER times a valve on the
   mountain-sized Great Pump and is flooded into its chamber.
3. **cand_03 — THE GRAFTS, the world that heals.** Grown chitin arches and spires
   that reseal any wound in seconds, so every opening RANGER cuts closes behind
   it; the cube is walled inside a nodule at the base of the healing Scar.
4. **cand_04 — THE MIGRATION, the walking continents.** Landmasses are colossal
   creature-plates striding across a silver powder sea; RANGER descends into the
   closing gap between two of them, and the whole herd halts and turns to face it.
5. **cand_05 — THE FLINCH, the reflex world.** A globe of tower-tall lilac fronds
   that slam flat in racing waves at any touch; RANGER must move in absolute
   stillness to reach the sealed Great Bud without making it clamp shut.
6. **cand_06 — ALBA, the world that defends itself.** Ivory flesh-plains studded
   with pearls; glassy defender swarms encase anything that moves, and RANGER is
   cocooned inside the Great Pearl and has to crack the fresh shell open.
7. **cand_07 — THE GULLET LANDS, the world that feeds.** Kilometre-wide amber
   funnels and ribbed conveyor plains slide all debris toward the throats; RANGER
   sprints up the moving floor to catch the cube before it goes down.
8. **cand_08 — THRUM, the thinking planet.** Filament causeways woven and
   unwoven by travelling thought-sparks; the world changes its mind mid-crossing,
   drops RANGER, then braids a new cable to lift it to the Knot.
9. **cand_09 — THE MOLT, the shedding planet.** Continent-sized grey shell plates
   peel off a raw iridescent new surface; RANGER is pinned under the Crown Plate
   as the old crust comes down, and the whole world sheds at once.
10. **cand_10 — THE SPAWNFIELDS, the world that grows terrain.** Plains of
    translucent bladders hatch mountains and canyons in seconds; the Brood Spire
    has grown itself around the cube like a seed.

## Pick: cand_01 — RESPIRA, the breathing planet

Chosen for the four criteria. Other-worldly: a planet whose mountains are muscle
and whose whole surface inflates and deflates on one shared breath is a single
impossible idea with no Earth analogue, and it is the safest cold open in the set
— a pale veined sphere visibly swelling and puffing spore columns can never be
mistaken for Earth, which is the failure mode this series has already paid for.
Visual: the strangeness is pure motion — ground rising hundreds of metres, vents
flaring, mist pillars surging — so it reads instantly in a vertical thirty-second
cut with no explanation. The challenge could only happen here: sprinting down the
throat of a breathing vent while concentric muscle rings squeeze shut in sequence
on the inhale, then dropping through the last gap on the exhale, is a timing
problem that exists nowhere but a world with lungs. Reaction beat: taking the cube
makes the entire planet inhale at once — every plain to the horizon swells and
every vent on the globe fires a white column skyward together — which is the
brief's "make it planetary" delivered in one shot. Runner-up was cand_04, whose
herd-halts-and-turns beat is the strongest single reaction in the batch, but its
orbit shot (continents on a sea) carries real Earth-resemblance risk.
