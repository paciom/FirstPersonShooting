# Episode 012 — SELECTION (domain: RESONANCE)

Hero: SAMURAI (cyan samurai robot with horned helmet). Counter: "FRAGMENT 012 / 512".
Spoken line: "Fragment twelve... secured."

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — The Chladni Plains.** The crust is one colossal vibrating plate; dust is shaken off every trembling zone and heaps along the still lines, engraving the whole planet with kilometre-wide geometric patterns that erase and redraw when the world's note changes.
2. **cand_02 — The Singing Erg.** A desert of black glass grains where a held note is what keeps a dune solid; sheets of sand hang in mid-air in standing ripples, and any pitch that dies turns hard ground into a sucking pit.
3. **cand_03 — The Tuning Fields.** A plain planted with millions of crystal tuning-fork prongs whose ringing shatters anything sharing its pitch, visible shock-rings of dust racing outward across whole continents.
4. **cand_04 — The Drumskin.** A hollow-crust world beaten from beneath by a planetary pulse that launches nested transparent shells of compressed air kilometres wide, stacked through the sky like slow glass domes.
5. **cand_05 — The Held Note.** Matter is held together by a tone from the core; in the acoustic shadows behind ridges, where no sound reaches, cliffs let go and dissolve into drifting powder.
6. **cand_06 — The Standing Sea.** A planet-wide liquid-metal ocean locked into a permanent standing wave: a regular grid of shivering silver spikes that go nowhere, flipping peaks-into-bowls the instant the pitch shifts.
7. **cand_07 — The Echo Basin.** A polished black bowl that never lets a sound go; every noise ever made still travels as a visible ring of lifted dust, bouncing off the walls and stacking into pressure walls where they converge.
8. **cand_08 — The Pipe Ranges.** Mountain ranges of hollow stone tubes played by a permanent gale, stone valves opening and sealing passages in rhythm, sounding pipes marked by columns of vapour visible from orbit.
9. **cand_09 — The Bell Fields.** A plain paved with vast thin slabs of ringing stone that float on their own vibration while they sound and sink back as the note dies, so the landscape is perpetually lifting and settling.
10. **cand_10 — The Rising Howl.** A world trapped in feedback: its tone climbs in visible cycles — dust jumping, gravel skating, cracks racing — until a plateau shatters, then falls back and begins again.

## Pick: cand_01 — The Chladni Plains

The central idea is the most other-worldly of the ten and, crucially, the only one that reads instantly from the cold-open orbit shot: a planet engraved edge to edge with enormous perfect geometry that visibly redraws itself needs no explanation at all, and it is impossible to mistake for Earth. The strangeness is entirely visual and cheap to render convincingly — heaped dust ridges, blurred buzzing ground, a rolling white erase — so the model is never asked to depict sound itself, only its debris. The challenge could exist nowhere else: safe footing is defined by a pattern, and the pattern rewrites itself mid-crossing, which gives the setback (the ridge under the robot's feet erased in an instant) and the recovery (riding a newly rising ridge inward) for free, without inventing a hazard. Its reaction beat is the strongest in the batch: the planet falls silent, every ridge collapses flat at once, then the whole world's dust leaps up and redraws itself into the exact glyph pattern etched on the cube — a planet-scale answer to a fist-sized object, tying the world's central image directly to the artifact.

Runners-up: cand_09 (Bell Fields) for the build-your-own-staircase-out-of-sound challenge, and cand_04 (Drumskin) for the nested pressure shells in flight.
