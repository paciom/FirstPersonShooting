A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic.

The hero: the sleek ice-white robot with the black racing-helmet visor from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is the second reference image.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. The robot drifts in orbit above a vast frozen planet ringed by FIVE moons of different sizes and colors, stacked across the sky in a diagonal line. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading "FRAGMENT 001 / 512". The robot looks down at the planet, then dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall the robot transforms into its fighter jet form and punches through the cloud layer. A sweeping, continuous flyover across the frozen world, each region more staggering than the last: an ocean of tsunami waves frozen mid-curl, glassy blue and hundreds of meters tall, the jet threading through the curl of one wave; then endless ice spires like an inverted chandelier forest, sparkling with diamond dust in the air; then a canyon where a waterfall froze solid mid-plunge, glowing turquoise from within; auroras in green and violet ribbon overhead while the five moons hang huge on the horizon. The camera alternates between the jet small against colossal landscapes and exhilarating wing-level tracking shots. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE LANDING (16-23s): the jet crests a final ridge revealing the episode's landmark: a perfectly circular frozen lake like a giant mirror, reflecting all five moons in a line. The jet transforms back into the robot and lands on the mirror-ice with a crouch. Deep in the ice below its feet, a faint golden light pulses. The robot presses one palm to the ice — and cracks spider out violently; the whole lake surface begins to split and tilt, huge ice plates rearing up. The robot leaps plate to plate as the lake shatters around it, sliding down a tilting slab toward the glow.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): at the heart of the broken lake, the golden cube — a fist-sized metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs — rises slowly out of the dark water on a column of light, rotating. The robot catches it in both hands; the glyphs flare and the aurora overhead bends toward it for one breathtaking beat.

THE RITUAL CLOSE (28-30s): cut to the robot's palm as the cube shoots upward and snaps into a vast floating holographic 8x8x8 lattice of 512 slots; exactly one slot ignites gold. A calm, epic voice speaks the episode's only words: "Fragment one... secured." Final frame holds on the lattice glowing against the stars.
