From above, the whole planet looks scribbled on.

TIME RULE: speed makes solid past-selves. The faster something moves, the more copies it leaves and the longer each one lasts before catching up and disappearing. The copies are real objects — they hold weight, block a path, and can be climbed on — but every one of them expires on a strict countdown, so anything built out of them must be used immediately.

Recovery: it swings up, accelerates along the rest of its own wake and leaps onto the Spire as the final copy blinks out behind it.
