A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: the white-and-orange robot with the black-and-white helmet and the wide red visor band from the first reference image — orange wing-fins on its shoulders and wheels built into its legs; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: one hard white sun lights everything; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing orange fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — the Stack: a world where time is layered by altitude. At ground level time barely moves — pale blue mist lies in perfectly still sheets and fine grit hangs unfallen in the air. The higher you climb the faster time runs, and you can SEE it: the enormous stone pillars rising from the plain are pristine and sharp-edged at their bases, weathered halfway up, and gnawed to thin twisted needles at their summits by centuries the ground below has never known. Above them the sky boils, its clouds tearing past so fast they smear.

TIME RULE: time speed increases smoothly with height and nothing else changes it. Every second the robot spends high up costs it years of wear — armor scouring, paint stripping — while at ground level it barely ages at all. Height is danger; the only way to survive the top is to spend as little time up there as possible, which means climbing and descending at full speed.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. The robot drifts in orbit above a pale world wrapped in two utterly mismatched skies — a high band of cloud whipping around the planet at visible speed, and beneath it a low haze that does not move at all, the two layers sliding against each other. Nothing about it resembles Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 006 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall the robot transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first through the screaming high sky, its hull frosting and dulling with age as it falls; it punches down through the layers, the racing clouds giving way to slower and slower air; it levels out and carves between the pillar bases through the still blue mist, wingtips cutting long clean scars in a fog that has not stirred in an age; then it climbs the flank of the greatest pillar, its skin visibly changing from smooth stone to a ruined lattice as the jet rises, and the landmark is revealed — the NEEDLE, the tallest pillar of all, its top storeys worn to a fragile spiral of thread-thin rock, glittering as it crumbles and sheds dust that falls forever more slowly as it goes down. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): the jet transforms back into the robot on a ledge partway up the Needle, and the ledge is already older than the ground. Above, the rock is dissolving in real time. The robot runs straight up the near-vertical spiral on its leg-wheels, going as fast as it can because every second up there strips it: paint burning off, one shoulder fin thinning to lace. A section of spiral ages out from under its feet and simply powders away. Setback. It falls, catches a rib of rock with one hand, swings, and rockets up the last stretch in a single unbroken sprint before the height can take any more.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): at the summit, standing in a shallow cup of ancient dust that the wind has polished for centuries, is the artifact from the reference image — a single fist-sized golden metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs, untouched and gleaming while everything around it has aged to nothing. The robot takes it. The glyphs flare — and for one breathtaking beat the whole Stack levels out: the racing sky above stops tearing, the frozen mist below rolls forward, and every pillar on the plain sheds a great sighing veil of dust that falls, at last, at one single speed.

Its glyphs pulse and golden holographic text appears in the air beside it reading exactly "FRAGMENT — SECURED" (this exact text, two words only, NO numbers or digits anywhere in it). A calm, epic voice speaks the episode's only words: "Fragment six... secured." Final frame holds on the robot and the single glowing cube, the falling dust drifting past it.
