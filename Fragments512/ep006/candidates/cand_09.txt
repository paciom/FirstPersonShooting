TIME RULE: which way time flows depends only on which side of the Seam you are on, and the effect is instant at the boundary. Anything crossing to the backward side has its own motion reversed and is dragged back the way it came. Directly ON the Seam the two directions cancel: a thread-thin ridge of raw white light where nothing ages and every footfall is erased the instant after it lands.
