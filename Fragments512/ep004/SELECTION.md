# Episode 004 — TITAN — domain: CRUSHING GRAVITY

Ten candidate worlds, all built on extreme or warped gravity, each with a
different landform, a different challenge mechanic and a different hiding place
for the cube. Titan's mass is a story beat in every one of them.

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — the Anvil.** A world crushed so flat nothing may stand, swept by
   visible gravity tides that pin Titan face-down until they pass; the cube is
   inside a compressed geode beneath the only slab left standing.
2. **cand_02 — the Spindle.** A planet tidally stretched into a teardrop with a
   glowing thread of molten stone streaming off its tip into space; near the tip
   "down" swings sideways then overhead, and the cube turns inside the thread.
3. **cand_03 — the Rising.** A world whose crust falls UPWARD into a ring made
   of its own rubble; Titan is the only thing heavy enough to stay on the ground,
   and rides a lifting slab to reach the cube in the ring's churning underside.
4. **cand_04 — the Well.** A boulder-sized shard of collapsed star buried in the
   crust has scooped a perfect thirty-kilometer bowl and bent the sky above it
   into rings of smeared starlight; the cube orbits the invisible point.
5. **cand_05 — the Pulse.** Gravity beats like a drum: whole boulder fields lift
   and slam in unison, horizon to horizon; Titan can move only in the light beats
   and the cube sits on a pillar that rises and crashes with the rhythm.
6. **cand_06 — the Drain.** An off-center dense core makes every slope on the
   planet lead to one place, where all the world's loose matter has stacked into
   a glowing kilometers-deep funnel; the cube is packed into its throat.
7. **cand_07 — the Roundstone Plain.** Wandering knots of gravity squeeze hills
   into mirror-smooth black spheres; Titan is caught and crushed between two of
   them, then cracks the biggest sphere open to find the cube in its hollow heart.
8. **cand_08 — the Slump.** Gravity too strong for any landform to hold its
   shape: the whole surface is a permanent collapse, and Titan must climb the
   last standing spire faster than the spire can fall out from under it.
9. **cand_09 — the Girdle.** A dense ring buried around the equator means "down"
   points sideways almost everywhere; dunes drape up cliff walls and the cube
   lies in the glowing knife-edge seam where everything eventually landed.
10. **cand_10 — the Hammerfall.** The world's gravity is shredding its dead
    companion and slamming the pieces down in a parallel rain of artillery; the
    cube is sealed in a bead of impact glass at the center of the strike zone.

## Pick: cand_02 — the Spindle

The central image is the most other-worldly of the ten and it explains itself in
under a second with no narration: a planet that is not a ball but a teardrop,
drawn to a fine point, bleeding a burning thread of its own stone into space.
That single silhouette carries the whole idea of crushing gravity in the cold
open, which is exactly what a vertical 30-second cut needs — the Anvil and the
Well are stranger to think about but flatter to look at, and the Well's key
object is literally invisible. The challenge could not exist anywhere else: on
the Spindle's tip gravity has rotated to point overhead, so Titan is not climbing
against a floor but hanging off a world that is trying to peel it away, and its
enormous weight — normally its advantage — is precisely what shears the slab
loose and nearly loses it. And the reaction beat is the biggest in the batch: the
instant the cube is taken, the thread ignites gold along its entire length, a
line of fire running from Titan's fist all the way into space, visible from the
orbit shot that opened the episode.
