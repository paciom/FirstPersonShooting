THE WORLD — the Girdle: a world whose gravity does not point at its center. Buried all the way around its middle is a dense ring of collapsed matter, glowing faintly through the crust in a thin unbroken line, and everything on this world falls toward that buried ring instead of toward the core. So "down" tilts as you travel: at the ring itself down is straight beneath your feet, but far from it down swings sideways, until near the poles the ground stands up like a wall and the true floor is the horizon. Dune fields drape UP the sides of mountains, spires grow out sideways, and vast slides of dust hang across cliff faces as if pasted there.

TILT RULE: everything falls toward the buried glowing ring, never toward the ground underfoot. On the tilted lands, dust runs across slopes instead of down them, loose stones roll sideways along cliffs and pile against the walls, and standing upright means leaning hard. Crossing onto the ring's own land swings down sharply through a quarter turn — and the heavier a thing is, the more violently the swing throws it.
