STRETCH RULE: gravity here does not point down — it points along the Spindle, toward the tip and the star beyond. The further out the robot goes, the more the pull swings from beneath its feet to sideways and finally to overhead, so cliff faces become floors and floors become walls. Nothing loose stays put: grit, shards and dust slide steadily outward along the needles and lift away into the thread. The heavier a thing is, the harder the stretch drags at it — and the robot is the heaviest thing on this world.
