# Episode 007 — candidate selection

Domain: THE INSIDE-OUT WORLD — worlds you enter and then fly around the inside
of; terrain curving UP and OVER the camera instead of falling to a horizon, a
single light hanging at the centre. Hero: KNIGHT. Counter: `FRAGMENT 007 / 512`.

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — the Hollow of Vess (The Hollow Lantern).** Enter through a polar
   mouth into a hollow planet of white terraces and rust dunes; the cube orbits
   a caged sun at the weightless dead centre, and taking it floods every
   direction of the inverted world with dawn at once.
2. **cand_02 — the Kel Band (The Torn Ring).** An abandoned ring megastructure
   whose land climbs into the sky ahead and behind; a colossal tear vents the
   whole structure's air into space, and the cube is wedged in the torn lip.
3. **cand_03 — Kaskal, the Double Hollow.** A hollow world with a second glowing
   world suspended inside it, stitched together by shearing columns; cross the
   middle and your weight reverses onto the inner sphere.
4. **cand_04 — the Needle Hollow.** Colossal mineral needles grow inward from
   every part of the shell, tips nearly meeting around a small ember; the robot
   leaps tip to tip and the cube waits in a broken-off tip drifting at the centre.
5. **cand_05 — Ashet, the Broken Hollow.** The shell has split into hundreds of
   curved plates that still hold the sphere's shape, starfield showing through
   the gaps; the gaps close on a slow rhythm and must be jumped.
6. **cand_06 — the Hanging Works.** A hollow world packed with alien machine
   spires that all hang inward, so the sky in every direction is architecture
   pointing at you; loose spires coast weightless through the centre and fall
   onto the far side, and the cube rides inside one of them.
7. **cand_07 — Halvent, the Drifting Day.** The central sun is loose and drifts,
   sweeping a moving oval of daylight across the inside of the world; unlit
   ground crumbles to powder, so the robot must outrun its own nightfall.
8. **cand_08 — the Long Hollow.** Not a planet but an abandoned tube longer than
   a continent, land wrapping a full 360° around the flight path, an axial light
   rod and a permanent axial wind; the cube sits behind the sealed End Iris.
9. **cand_09 — the Weave.** A planet-sized ball of woven alloy beams with no
   solid shell; starfield shows through the slots, gravity flips from beam to
   beam, and the cube is cradled inside a sliding Knot of a dozen crossings.
10. **cand_10 — Orun, the Inner Drift.** Thousands of loose slabs of land orbit
    the central bead inside the hollow world like a storm of countries; the cube
    rides a mountain-sized slab already grinding into the inner shell.

## Pick: cand_06 — the Hanging Works

The most other-worldly of the ten and the one that reads fastest without a word
of explanation: a sky made entirely of colossal spires hanging down at the
camera from ground that curves up and over on all sides says "inside-out world"
in a single frame, and it holds up in a narrow vertical crop where a wide
curving vista would not. Its physics rule — a spire breaks loose, falls inward,
coasts weightless through the centre and then falls onto the OPPOSITE side of
the world — is a mechanic no other kind of world can produce, and it drives a
challenge that is pure inside-out: the robot rides a drifting spire through the
weightless middle, and as the spire leaves the centre its weight comes back with
"down" flipped end for end and drops it the length of the shaft. The reaction
beat is the strongest in the batch because it uses the geometry: light races out
from the Hub and ignites a million hanging spires in a wave that passes over the
camera and closes around the far side, so the audience sees the whole enclosed
world at once. cand_01 is the purest statement of the domain and cand_02 the
most dramatic single landmark, but neither turns the curvature itself into the
obstacle the way this one does.
