The hero: the orange, white and black knight robot with cyan trim from the first reference image — an angular horned helm with glowing cyan eyes, heavy black gauntlets and boots; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. LIGHTING RULE: the only light is the Rod — one continuous bar of white light running down the middle of the tube for its entire length, hanging in mid-air with nothing holding it. Everything is lit from that central line; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — the Long Hollow: not a planet at all but an abandoned alien tube, a spinning cylinder longer than a continent with all of its land on the INSIDE. Fly down the middle and the terrain wraps completely around you — silver-green terraces below, more terraces to the left and right, and the same terraces upside down directly overhead, curving without a break, so the world is a tunnel of country with no horizon and no sky. Down the axis runs the Rod, and at the far end the tube closes in a colossal iris of interlocking plates, shut for a very long time.

PHYSICS RULE: the tube's spin presses everything outward onto the inner wall, so "down" points at the nearest terrace whichever way the robot turns, and the land always closes over the camera. The spin also drives a permanent river of wind along the axis, endlessly circling the tube; loose dust and ribbons of vapour ride that wind in long spiral streaks around the Rod. Anything that leaves the wall drifts toward the axis, gets caught in the wind and is carried the length of the world.
