A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: the sleek orange PANTHER robot from the first reference image — a dark navy angular predator visor with a single yellow eye-slit, and glowing cyan panels running across its limbs; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: this world has NO sun and NO moons. The single dominant light source is the glowing rock itself — canyon walls made of radiant mineral strata that shine on their own in bands of magenta, amber and deep green. Light therefore comes SIDEWAYS out of the walls, from both sides at once, edge-lighting everything in coloured rim light with a black sky overhead and black shadow in the middle of the canyon floor. Keep this side-glow consistent in every shot. Panther's cyan panels and yellow eye-slit are the only moving light.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — THE GLOWVEIN CANYONS: a sunless rogue planet whose crust is layered like a cut gemstone, band on band of self-luminous mineral laid down over ages. Erosion has sliced this crust into a continent-wide maze of narrow canyons, and every cut wall glows: horizontal stripes of magenta, amber and green stacked hundreds of metres deep, so flying down a canyon is like flying down the inside of a lit rainbow trench. Above the canyon rims there is only dead black rock and hard stars — all the light in this world is buried, and only the cuts let it out.

MINERAL RULE: the glow lives in the exposed rock face. Fresh breaks blaze bright; anything covered — by dust, by rockfall, by drifting grit — goes dark and stays dark. The deeper the canyon, the older and brighter the bands, and the deepest band of all burns pure gold.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. Panther drifts in orbit above a black planet scored all over with glowing coloured scratches, as if something had raked the world open and light had leaked out of the wounds, visibly NOT Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 005 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall Panther transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first over the rim into the maze. A sweeping continuous flyover down the glowing trenches: threading a narrow magenta-banded slot, wingtips a metre from each wall, the colours strobing across the fuselage; bursting through a curtain of dark falling grit into a wide amber chamber; carving a fast helix down a spiral shaft where the bands wind around the walls like a lit thread; then dropping deeper and deeper until the walls turn pure gold and open onto the landmark — THE VAULT, a colossal circular chasm at the maze's heart where every colour band in the planet's crust converges into concentric glowing rings around a black floor far below. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): the jet transforms back into the robot and descends the Vault's rings toward the black floor. Its landing shakes the wall and a huge slide of dark grit pours down, burying the glowing bands as it goes — the light dying rank by rank, the chasm going out around it like lamps being covered. Setback: Panther is left in near-total black, the grit still rising, the exit lost. Recovery: it drags its claws hard along the buried wall, scraping a long bright stripe of fresh glowing mineral back open — a line of light it draws for itself — and climbs along that stripe, cutting more glow out of the rock with every reach.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): the fresh scratch leads it to a sealed pocket in the gold band, and inside, resting on a column of light, is the artifact from the reference image — a single fist-sized golden metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs, its glyph light and the gold band matching exactly. Panther lifts it free. The glyphs flare — and the reaction races out through the crust: every buried band in the planet lights through its covering of dust, the whole maze igniting at once, thousands of kilometres of canyon flaring magenta-amber-green-gold in a single spreading pulse until the entire dark world is drawn in glowing lines.

THE RITUAL CLOSE (28-30s): close-up on the robot's open palm holding the ONE cube — there is only ever this single cube on screen. Its glyphs pulse and golden holographic text appears in the air beside it reading exactly "FRAGMENT — SECURED" (this exact text, two words only, NO numbers or digits anywhere in it). A calm, epic voice speaks the episode's only words: "Fragment five... secured." Final frame holds on Panther's visor and the single glowing cube, the blazing coloured canyon walls behind it.
