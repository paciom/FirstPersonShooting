A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: the sleek orange PANTHER robot from the first reference image — a dark navy angular predator visor with a single yellow eye-slit, and glowing cyan panels running across its limbs; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: this world has NO sun and NO moons. The single dominant light source is COLD FIRE — a running line of blue-white chemical flame that burns wherever the liquid of the sea touches dry shore. The light is therefore always a thin bright LINE low on the ground, lighting everything from below and from the side, throwing long shadows away from the waterline. Keep this line the only light in every shot. Panther's cyan panels and yellow eye-slit are the only other glow.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — BRINELIGHT: a rogue planet with no star, covered in black basins of a strange cold liquid. The liquid does not burn on its own — but the shore mineral does, and where the two meet the ground ignites in a cold blue-white flame that gives light without heat. The tide is enormous and never rests, so every shoreline on this world is a moving line of blue fire, drawing and redrawing the map of the land in light while everything away from the water stays absolutely black.

TIDE RULE: light exists only exactly where liquid meets dry ground. Where the tide advances the flame line advances with it; where the liquid has already covered the ground, the flame goes out and that area goes dark. The flames are COLD — they light everything brilliantly and burn nothing.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. Panther drifts in orbit above a black planet outlined entirely in thin glowing blue threads — the burning coastlines of a hundred dark seas, shifting and rewriting themselves as the tides move, visibly NOT Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 005 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall Panther transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first toward the burning coastlines. A sweeping continuous flyover along the fire-lines: racing a mile above a blue burning shore that snakes to the horizon; dropping to skim the black liquid so the jet's wake tears a temporary line of flame across the surface behind it; banking through a maze of flooded terraces where each step of the terrace burns its own bright rim, a staircase of light; then pulling up to reveal the landmark — THE COLD CATARACT, a vast waterfall where the sea pours off a cliff onto a mineral shelf below and burns in mid-air the whole way down, a curtain of blue-white cold flame taller than anything else in the world. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): the jet transforms back into the robot on a black shoal in the pool below the cataract, where the sea is retreating and the burning line is running outward across the flats. Panther prowls after the light, following the only lit ground on the planet — and then the tide turns. The line reverses and comes back at it fast, the black liquid swallowing the shoal, the light going out behind it as the flame line closes in. Setback: it is cut off, standing on a shrinking island of dark rock with the flame line racing past on both sides. Recovery: Panther sprints and leaps ONTO the burning line itself, running along the cold flame like a lit road — it does not burn — and rides it to the cataract wall.

Panther steps through the flame and lifts it. The glyphs flare — and the reaction runs around the planet: every sea at once surges up its shore, and every coastline on the world ignites along its full length in the same heartbeat, a global net of blue-white fire flashing into being from horizon to horizon.
