LIGHTING RULE: this world has NO sun, NO moons and NO light of its own at all. The single dominant light source is PANTHER ITSELF — the cyan panels on its limbs, the yellow slit of its visor, and the blue fire of its engines. Light always radiates outward from the robot, falls off fast into absolute black, and travels with it. Everything visible is visible because the robot is there. Keep this consistent in every shot; the frame is mostly black, and the robot is always crisp and readable.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — NULLREACH: a rogue planet that fell out of its system so long ago that no light has touched it in an age. There is no glow in the ground, no fire below, no storm above — only a vast dune sea of fine black glass dust, sculpted into towering razor-edged ridges, and a sky of nothing but hard distant stars. The dust is retro-reflective: it throws light straight back at whatever lit it, so Panther's cyan glow paints a sharp cone of glittering ground ahead and the world beyond the cone simply does not exist.

DARK RULE: nothing on this world emits light except the robot. Distance is unreadable — a ridge two steps away and a ridge a kilometre away look identical in the glow. Panther's predator visor sees HEAT instead: in visor-view shots the world reads as a cold blue-black wireframe with warm shapes standing out, and this is the only way to judge what is solid and what is empty air.
