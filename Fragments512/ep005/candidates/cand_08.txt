A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: the sleek orange PANTHER robot from the first reference image — a dark navy angular predator visor with a single yellow eye-slit, and glowing cyan panels running across its limbs; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: this world has NO sun and NO moons. The single dominant light source is a flying RIVER OF LIGHT — an immense migrating swarm of tiny glowing airborne drift-creatures, pale gold, that pours across the sky in a dense braided stream. The light is therefore soft, golden and always overhead-and-moving, sliding across the terrain as the swarm passes; behind the swarm the land is pitch black. Keep the swarm as the only light in every shot. Panther's cyan panels and yellow eye-slit are the only other glow.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — THE SPARKRUN: a rogue planet without a star, whose only living light gets up and travels. Countless tiny luminous drift-creatures, each no bigger than a seed, migrate together in one continuous braided river of gold that flows endlessly around the planet a few hundred metres above the ground. Under the river the land is beautiful — black pillar-mesas, dry basins, wind-carved arches. Ahead of the river and behind it, the same land is invisible. The world has exactly one bright place at a time, and it is always moving on.

SWARM RULE: the drift-creatures are drawn to steady cool light and repelled by sudden heat or noise. Panther's cyan panels attract them — they gather and thicken around it — while a hard engine burn scatters them instantly, and a scattered swarm takes long seconds to reform, leaving everything black.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall Panther transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first into the golden river. A sweeping continuous flyover inside the light: flying THROUGH the swarm as the glowing motes part around the nose and stream over the canopy in glittering sheets; dropping below it to race across lit black pillar-mesas whose shadows swing as the river slides overhead; carving through a wind-arch just as the swarm pours through it too, filling the arch with gold; then banking wide to reveal the landmark — THE ROOST, a single colossal hollow rock tower where the river dips down and spirals into an opening in its side, so the whole tower breathes glowing motes in and out in a slow golden whirlpool. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): the jet transforms back into the robot and enters the Roost through the whirlpool mouth. Inside, the swarm coils in a spiral column, lighting the hollow tower from its centre. Setback: a ledge gives way, it catches itself with a burst of engine flare — and the heat SCATTERS the swarm, the entire tower plunging into total darkness with the robot clinging to a wall it can no longer see. Recovery: Panther goes utterly still, dims everything but the cyan panels, and lets the motes drift back and settle on it — the light rebuilds around the robot itself, brighter than before, and it climbs on.

Panther reaches in and takes it. The glyphs flare — and the reaction spans the planet: every swarm on the world turns at once and pours toward the Roost from every horizon, a hundred golden rivers converging into one immense pillar of light that stands from the ground to the black sky.
