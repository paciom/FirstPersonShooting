The hero: the sleek orange PANTHER robot from the first reference image — a dark navy angular predator visor with a single yellow eye-slit, and glowing cyan panels running across its limbs; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. LIGHTING RULE: this world has NO sun and NO moons. The single dominant light source is the GEYSERS — towering vertical pillars of white-hot glowing gas that erupt one after another across the plain. Only one or two pillars burn at any moment, so the lit direction changes as the eruption chain walks across the land — always name and follow the burning pillar in shot. Panther's cyan panels and yellow eye-slit are the only other light.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — THE BREATHING FLATS: a rogue planet with no star, its surface an endless pale ash plain punched with thousands of dark vent cones. Deep pressure drives glowing superheated gas up through the vents, and because the vents share the same plumbing, they fire in a CHAIN — one erupts, then its neighbour, then the next, a line of blazing white pillars marching across the flats like a wave of lamps switching on and off. Between eruptions the plain is dead black.

VENT RULE: the chain always moves in one direction and never fires the same cone twice in a row. A cone about to blow first sighs a thin ring of glowing dust around its mouth — a warning, a second or two ahead. Standing over a cone when it fires means being thrown into the sky on a column of light.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. Panther drifts in orbit above a black planet where thin bright lines of light stitch themselves across the dark surface and vanish, over and over, like sparks running along fuses, visibly NOT Earth. THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall Panther transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first toward a chain of firing pillars. A sweeping continuous flyover, racing the eruption line: flying low along the chain so pillar after pillar blazes up just behind the tail, each one throwing the jet's shadow a kilometre ahead across the ash; slicing straight through a dying pillar in a burst of glowing vapour; banking around a cluster of cones that fire together into a ring of light; then climbing to reveal the landmark — THE GREAT MOUTH, one immense vent cone the size of a mountain, dormant and dark, its rim ringed with the glowing dust of a hundred smaller vents breathing around its base. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): the jet transforms back into the robot on the flats and must cross the last stretch to the Great Mouth on foot, through the chain. Panther prowls low and fast, reading the glowing warning rings and timing every sprint between eruptions, pillars slamming up on either side and lighting it in flashes of white. Setback: it misreads a ring, a cone fires directly under it and hurls it spinning into the black sky. Recovery: mid-flight, high above the plain and lit from below by the pillar that threw it, Panther twists, kicks off the rising gas column with both feet and drops in a controlled dive straight into the Great Mouth's rim.

Panther seizes it just as the Mouth's rim begins to sigh. The glyphs flare — and the reaction breaks: every vent on the planet fires at the same instant, thousands of white pillars standing up together from horizon to horizon, turning the whole black plain into a field of light with the robot rising on the greatest pillar of all.
