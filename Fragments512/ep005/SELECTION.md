# Episode 005 — candidate selection

Domain: THE LIGHTLESS WORLD (no sun; everything visible is lit from within).
Hero: PANTHER. Counter: `FRAGMENT 005 / 512`. Spoken line: "Fragment five... secured."

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — GLOWMOOR.** A living luminous mat carpets the whole planet and dims wherever it is touched, so standing still means going blind; the cube is sealed inside a giant cold-locked bloom that only opens to Panther's body heat.
2. **cand_02 — THE ANVIL.** A permanent lightning storm is the only light over a mirror-flat black glass plain; Panther must snuff its own cyan panels and climb a strike-magnet spire blind, moving on the afterimage of each flash.
3. **cand_03 — THE CINDER LATTICE.** A black crust cracked into drifting plates with molten seams between them, so the planet is a world-sized circuit of orange light that opens and closes under the robot's weight.
4. **cand_04 — NULLREACH.** A world with zero light of its own: Panther's cyan panels and visor are the only lamp in existence, depth is unreadable, and the cube is the single point of gold visible from orbit.
5. **cand_05 — VEILFALL.** One colossal ground-touching aurora curtain is the only light and it slowly walks across the planet, so Panther has to race the light up a magnetic spire before darkness overtakes it.
6. **cand_06 — BRINELIGHT.** Cold blue flame burns only exactly where a black sea touches dry mineral shore, making every coastline a moving line of fire; cut off by the turning tide, Panther runs along the flame line itself.
7. **cand_07 — THE GLOWVEIN CANYONS.** The crust is layered self-luminous mineral and only cut canyon walls let the light out; buried by a grit slide, Panther claws a fresh stripe of glowing rock open and climbs the line it drew.
8. **cand_08 — THE SPARKRUN.** The only light is alive and migrating — a braided river of glowing drift-motes flowing around the planet; a panicked engine flare scatters the swarm and Panther must stand still and let the light come back to it.
9. **cand_09 — THE BREATHING FLATS.** Chained geyser vents fire in sequence across an ash plain, so the light is a marching wave of white pillars; Panther times its sprint by the warning rings and gets thrown skyward by a mistimed vent.
10. **cand_10 — THE BLACKMERE.** Ancient sunken alien machine cores glow up through a thick tar sea and are dying out one by one; the tar holds only while Panther keeps moving, and the last core is the cube's vault.

## Pick: cand_03 — THE CINDER LATTICE

The central idea is the most instantly legible of the ten: one frame of a black plain webbed with glowing orange seams explains the whole world with no narration, and the orbit shot — a lightless sphere drawn over in a shifting web of fire — cannot possibly be mistaken for Earth, which is the failure mode this series keeps hitting. Its vocabulary is entirely non-Earth (plates, seams, melt, petals), so there is no die-roll toward galleons, forests or coastlines the way the sea and swarm candidates carry. The challenge could only exist here: the floor itself tips under the robot's weight, and every tip both threatens it and opens a new blade of light, so danger and illumination are the same mechanic — Panther's cyan panels and predator visor stay the only cool light and the only readable thing in an underlit frame. And the reaction beat is the strongest in the batch: every seam on the planet flashing from orange to white-gold turns the whole world into an igniting circuit, rhyming exactly with the circuit glyphs on the cube being lifted.
