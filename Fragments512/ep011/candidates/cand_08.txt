A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: the blue-and-gold robot with the gold horn crest and the gold wing-blades on its back, from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: one distant blue-white sun above the cloud tops lights everything from high behind, so the sinking giant below is always a dark shape with a bright rim; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — the SINKING COLOSSUS: an immense grey-green gas world with no surface at any depth, only ever-thicker air. Falling through it, slowly and forever, is a derelict alien machine the size of a mountain range — an ancient framework of black metal ribs and dead engine towers, built by something long gone, so vast that it drags its own weather down with it: a permanent bowl of spiralling cloud pressed under its belly and a kilometres-wide wake of torn vapour trailing up behind it. It has been sinking for ages and it will never land, because there is nothing down there to land on. It only gets deeper, and the deeper it goes the more the air crushes.

DESCENT RULE: the colossus falls steadily and everything near it falls with it, dragged down in its wake; the air below is thicker, darker and heavier every second, so time spent on the machine is time spent sinking. Nothing else in the sky holds still — there is no ground, no rock, no shore, only the falling machine and the cloud pouring around it.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. The robot drifts in orbit above a grey-green banded world with a single strange dark scar of disturbed cloud spiralling into its depths, as if something enormous had fallen in — visibly NOT Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 011 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall the robot transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first down the wake. A sweeping continuous flyover: first through the high wake, ribbons of torn vapour rushing upward past the wings; then into the shadow it casts, where the light dims and the black machine begins to appear out of the murk piece by piece — a rib here, a dead engine tower there, each bigger than a mountain; then a long low pass along the machine's flank, dark metal blurring beneath the jet for kilometres; and then the reveal — the whole of it seen at last from below and ahead as the clouds part, a colossal dark alien machine falling through a bowl of spiralling cloud, and one small gold light burning in a gap between its ribs. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): the jet transforms back into the robot and lands on the machine — the only thing to stand on in this entire world, and it is falling. It runs along a rib toward the gold light while the air darkens and thickens around it, the pressure fogging and frosting its armour. A rib gives way and the robot drops off the edge into the pouring dark beneath the machine, tumbling with nothing below it forever. It flares its gold wing-blades, banks into the machine's own downdraft, and rides that rushing wind back up along the flank and in through the gap between the ribs.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): inside the ribs, in a dead chamber of ancient alien metal, the artifact from the reference image rests on a cracked plinth — a single fist-sized golden metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs. The robot lifts it. The glyphs flare, and for one breathtaking beat the whole colossus lights up along every rib and tower with golden lines, and — for the first time in ages — it STOPS falling, hanging still in the deep as the clouds keep pouring past.
