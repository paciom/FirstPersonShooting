THE WORLD — the SHEAR SEAM: a titanic gas world with no solid ground at any depth, whose stacked decks race past one another in opposite directions at unimaginable speed. Where two decks meet, the air tears: an endless straight seam wraps the whole world, and along it the torn air curls into a line of colossal rolling barrel-vortices — horizontal spinning cylinders of cloud, each many kilometres across, turning like rollers between the two decks and stretching away to the horizon in both directions. Above, cream cloud streams left; below, rust cloud streams right; between them the great rollers turn forever.

SHEAR RULE: everything near the seam is being dragged. The upper deck pulls one way, the lower deck the other, so a flyer crossing the seam is thrown hard sideways and must fight to keep its nose straight. The rollers spin steadily; anything caught on one is carried around and around with it, over the top and under the bottom, and cannot simply climb off — it must leave at the top of the turn or not at all.
