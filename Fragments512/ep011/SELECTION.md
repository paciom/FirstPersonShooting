# Episode 011 — candidate selection

Domain: THE DEPTHS OF A GIANT — a colossal gas world with no solid ground at
any depth. Hero: HAWK (blue-and-gold, gold horn crest, gold wing-blades).
Counter: `FRAGMENT 011 / 512`. Voice line: "Fragment eleven... secured."

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — the GLASSFALL.** Gemstones condense out of the air and fall
   forever, growing bigger the deeper they go; the cube is inside the Anvil
   Gem, a mountain-sized crystal falling through the dark.
2. **cand_02 — the STORMSPINE.** A chain of hollow whirlwind shafts linked like
   vertebrae, each spinning opposite the last; you descend only when their
   mouths briefly align, and the cube hangs in the dead calm of the deepest one.
3. **cand_03 — the DRIFT GARDENS.** Colossal translucent gasbag creatures
   (skryn) hold their own depths in an endless living sky; the cube is in the
   crystal ballast-heart of the deepest, largest elder.
4. **cand_04 — the QUICKSILVER DEEP.** The bottom layer is a horizonless mirror
   of liquid metal that swallows anything that rests on it; the cube lies out on
   the open mirror and only speed keeps the robot from sinking.
5. **cand_05 — the UNDERLIGHT.** A sunless world lit from below by its own
   molten depths, so shadows point upward and it gets brighter as you dive; the
   cube is inside a coalshade, a cold black cloud where the updraft dies.
6. **cand_06 — the THOUSAND PANES.** Hundreds of razor-sharp stacked layers with
   mirror-flat boundaries you punch through in splash-rings; the cube floats
   pinned against the underside of the deepest pane like a bubble under glass.
7. **cand_07 — the SHEAR SEAM.** Two decks race past each other in opposite
   directions and tear the air into an endless line of rolling barrel-vortices;
   the cube rides the flank of the greatest roller, flashing once per rotation.
8. **cand_08 — the SINKING COLOSSUS.** An ancient derelict alien machine the
   size of a mountain range falls forever through the decks, dragging its own
   weather down with it; the cube waits inside its ribs as the pressure rises.
9. **cand_09 — the BELLOWS.** The whole atmosphere swells and squeezes on a
   steady pulse, lifting and crushing entire decks; the cube sits in the eye of
   a ring-cloud that opens only at the top of each swell.
10. **cand_10 — the UPWELL.** One hundred-kilometre column of hot amber gas
    rises out of the unreachable deep; the cube is climbing it, and the robot
    must fly DOWN the throat against the flood to catch it before it is flung
    out of the world.

## Pick: **cand_06 — the THOUSAND PANES**

One-sentence pitch: a gas giant built of hundreds of razor-edged stacked layers
whose mirror-flat boundaries can be punched through but never rested on, where
the fragment floats trapped against the underside of the deepest pane like a
bubble under glass.

Reasoning. It is the most *visual* idea in the batch and the one that reads
instantly in a vertical frame: a 9:16 shot is a column, and this world is a
column of hard-edged colour bands the jet punches down through one splash-ring
at a time, so the audience feels depth accumulating without a word of
explanation. The challenge could not happen anywhere else — the robot is flipped
by buoyancy and pinned upside down against a black glass ceiling with a
bottomless drop below it, unable to stand and unable to fall, and has to crawl
along the underside of the sky; that single image is the whole domain (nowhere
to land, and the cube is DEEP) in one shot, and Hawk's wing-blades are what
solve it. The reaction beat is the strongest of the ten because it is
world-scale but simple to render: hundreds of golden rings racing outward on
hundreds of stacked layers, straight up to the sun. Runners-up were cand_05
(the inverted-light world is more alien but risks the model reintroducing a sun)
and cand_10 (best flight-mastery challenge, but its landmark is a single column
rather than a whole world).
