# Episode 010 — SCOUT — candidate selection

Domain: IMPOSSIBLE GEOMETRY. Counter "FRAGMENT 010 / 512", voice line
"Fragment ten... secured."

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — the Reiteration**: every mountain is built out of smaller copies
   of the same mountain, forever, so nothing has a knowable size and the ledge
   you land on turns out to be a bump on a bigger version of itself.
2. **cand_02 — the Tessellation**: a small world that repeats — the same mesa
   tiled in a perfect grid to every horizon, and flying fast enough Scout
   catches up with the back of its own jet.
3. **cand_03 — the Ribbon**: the planet is a colossal band of land with one
   half-twist, so it has only one surface; travel far enough and you come home
   standing upside-down under your own footprints.
4. **cand_04 — the Knot**: the world is a single rope of desert tied in a knot,
   threading through its own coils, with starfield showing through the gaps and
   another continent hanging overhead.
5. **cand_05 — the Impossible Arch**: three continent-long stone beams joined
   into a triangle whose corners meet at angles that cannot close but do, with
   down turning ninety degrees at every join.
6. **cand_06 — the Descending Loop**: a terraced world where every path leads
   downhill and still returns above where it started, and cataracts of dust
   fall forever into their own source.
7. **cand_07 — the Unfixed**: a world where size will not hold still — pebbles
   swell into mountains as you approach and shrink again as you pass, and the
   robot is the only fixed-size thing on the planet.
8. **cand_08 — the Pleat**: the crust is one sheet folded back on itself into
   hundreds of vertical leaves packed edge to edge, with closing slots of sky
   between them.
9. **cand_09 — the Scroll**: a planet that never closed — its crust wound in an
   open spiral so there are three more landscapes stacked in the sky above you,
   raining grit down on the one you stand on.
10. **cand_10 — the Orthogon**: a planet that is a perfect six-sided block,
    straight-line horizons, airless edges, and down swinging ninety degrees
    every time you cross one.

## Pick: cand_01 — the Reiteration

It is the only candidate whose impossibility lands in a *single still frame*
with zero setup: a ridge visibly made of the same ridge, made of the same
ridge, made of the same ridge, all lit by one sun — a viewer scrolling past on
a phone reads it instantly and cannot mistake it for anywhere real, which the
looping and folding worlds (cand_02, cand_03, cand_08) only achieve after a
beat of travel. The challenge could exist nowhere else: Scout lands, the ledge
shivers and reveals itself as one small copy on the flank of a far larger
identical slope, and the robot has to outrun a mountain that keeps growing
under it — pure recon-and-speed, exactly Scout's job, and unsolvable by
strength. The fragment beat is the strongest of the ten: the gold repeats
outward through every size of the world at once, racing to the horizon and
inward to nothing, a chain of light that shows the whole recursion in two
seconds. It also carries no production risk — unlike cand_10, whose cube-shaped
planet fights the "only ever this single cube on screen" rule in the close-up,
and unlike cand_04's gravity flip, which brushes against episode 004's domain.
