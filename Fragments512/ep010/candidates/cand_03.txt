THE WORLD — the Ribbon: this world is not a ball. It is a single colossal band of land, hundreds of kilometres wide and endlessly long, curving through space in a great closed loop with one half-twist in it. Because of that twist the band has only ONE surface: travel far enough along the top and you arrive back at your starting point standing on the underside, upside-down, with the sun now beneath your feet. Terrain runs unbroken over the edge and around: pale bone-coloured dunes and turquoise mineral rivers pour over the rim and continue on the other face without a break.

ONE-SIDED RULE: the band has no top and no bottom — gravity always pulls toward the nearest surface of the ribbon, so the robot can stand anywhere on it, including apparently upside-down, with open starfield below. Terrain, dust and light must flow continuously around the edges. The half-twist is always visible somewhere in the distance as the band rolls over on itself.
