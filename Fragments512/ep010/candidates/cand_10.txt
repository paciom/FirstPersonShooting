The hero: SCOUT, the sleek ice-white robot with the black racing-helmet visor from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. LIGHTING RULE: one distant white sun lights everything; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — the Orthogon: a planet with flat faces and straight edges. This world is a perfect six-sided block hanging in space, its faces broad plains of slate and pale sulphur, its edges razor-straight ridges thousands of kilometres long, its corners bare rock spearing out of the atmosphere into vacuum. Because the pull is toward the block's centre, each flat face behaves like a shallow bowl — dust and thin cloud pool at the middle of every face and thin away to nothing at the edges — and the horizon is not a curve but a hard straight line with stars directly beyond it.

BLOCK RULE: the world is one solid block, never rounded. Cross an edge and the direction of down turns ninety degrees at once: what was a wall becomes a floor, and the terrain that looked vertical from one face lies flat from the next. Atmosphere is thick and hazy in the middle of each face and absent at the edges and corners, so the ridges stand naked against black space.
