A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: SCOUT, the sleek ice-white robot with the black racing-helmet visor from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: one distant red-gold sun lights everything; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — the Descending Loop: a world made entirely of terraces, and every terrace leads downhill. Cut into the whole planet are colossal stepped canyons of layered ochre stone, and each flight of steps descends — yet follow any of them far enough and it delivers you to the top of the flight you started on, higher than where you began, without ever once going up. Falling dust behaves the same way: great pale cataracts of powder pour endlessly down the terraces, run around four sides of a canyon, and spill back over their own source at the top, falling forever in a closed circuit.

DOWNHILL RULE: every path, step and cataract on this world visibly slopes DOWNWARD, always, in every shot — and every route still returns to its own starting height after four turns. Never show a path climbing. Water-like powder falls down and arrives above itself; the loop must be readable in a single wide shot with all four descending sides visible at once.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. SCOUT drifts in orbit above an ochre world with no mountains at all — its whole surface a vast pattern of stepped rectangular canyons, pale cataracts of dust pouring down every side and somehow feeding their own tops — visibly NOT Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 010 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall SCOUT transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first into the terraces. A sweeping continuous flyover, always descending: chasing a cataract of white powder down a thousand steps; banking around a corner into a second flight that also drops away below; carving a third and a fourth, the sun holding steady while the world's steps keep falling — and then the reveal, the jet pulling up to show the landmark: the Cascade Ring, four descending canyons joined in a square, one endless cataract pouring down all four of them at once and arriving back at its own beginning in mid-air, a curtain of falling dust that never runs out. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): the jet transforms back into SCOUT on the terraces beside the Cascade Ring, where the cube's glow shines from a ledge plainly BELOW it — but every set of steps down brings the robot back to the same terrace, four times over, the glow still below and never nearer. It sprints down the loop, faster each lap, the falling powder hammering the steps around it. On the third lap a terrace crumbles and SCOUT drops through the cataract, blinded and tumbling — it slams its hand into the step face, holds, and then instead of following the stairs it launches straight off the edge, across the open middle of the ring.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): SCOUT lands on the ledge at the centre of the Cascade Ring, the one place the loop never passes, and there behind the falling curtain of dust, on a thin column of light, rests the artifact from the reference image: a single fist-sized golden metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs. SCOUT takes it. The glyphs flare, and one breathtaking beat fires: every cataract on the planet stops falling for one held beat, hanging as a still curtain of powder — and then reverses, the loop finally releasing, dust lifting off the terraces in golden sheets to every horizon.

THE RITUAL CLOSE (28-30s): close-up on SCOUT's open palm holding the ONE cube — there is only ever this single cube on screen. Its glyphs pulse and golden holographic text appears in the air beside it reading exactly "FRAGMENT — SECURED" (this exact text, two words only, NO numbers or digits anywhere in it). A calm, epic voice speaks the episode's only words: "Fragment ten... secured." Final frame holds on the ice-white robot and the single glowing cube.
