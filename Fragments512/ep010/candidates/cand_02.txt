The hero: SCOUT, the sleek ice-white robot with the black racing-helmet visor from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. LIGHTING RULE: one distant amber sun lights everything; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — the Tessellation: a world that repeats. Fly far enough in a straight line and you do not curve around a globe, you simply arrive back where you started, coming in behind yourself. The landscape is one small patch of black-glass mesas and copper salt flats, tiled endlessly in a perfect square grid to every horizon: the same mesa, the same crack, the same wind-scar, repeated over and over into the distance like a printed pattern. Because the world repeats, a traveller flying fast enough can see the back of their own jet in the distance ahead.

REPEATING-SHAPE RULE: every horizon shows an exact grid of identical copies of the same terrain patch, aligned in perfect rows, all lit identically by the one sun. Copies are never mirrored, never varied. Anything that leaves one edge of a tile instantly enters the opposite edge — dust, contrails and the robot itself.

Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.
