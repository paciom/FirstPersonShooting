A 30-second cinematic episode of an epic space-exploration saga, awe-inspiring and breathtaking, like a nature documentary crossed with a sci-fi epic. This is an ALIEN place — nothing about it should resemble Earth.

The hero: SCOUT, the sleek ice-white robot with the black racing-helmet visor from the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is shown from several angles in the following reference images — note the sharp pointed nose at the front and the engine exhausts at the rear. The golden glyph cube artifact is the last reference image.

LIGHTING RULE: one distant silver-blue sun lights everything; consistent light direction and shadows in every shot. No moons.

FLIGHT RULE: whenever the jet flies, it always flies NOSE-FIRST — the sharp pointed nose leads in the direction of travel, engines trailing blue fire. It banks and carves like a real aircraft, never drifting sideways or backwards.

THE WORLD — the Unfixed: a world where size will not hold still. Nothing here has one true scale. A pebble of blue-grey stone lying at the robot's feet becomes, as the robot walks toward it, a mesa the height of a mountain range; keep walking and it is a pebble again, sitting on a plain of identical pebbles that are themselves mountains from further off. Spires swell into continents as you approach and shrink to splinters as you pass. The land is the same land throughout — only its size refuses to agree with itself.

SCALE RULE: objects change size relative to the viewer as the distance to them changes, smoothly and continuously, so perspective never behaves normally — things approached grow far too fast or shrink instead of growing, while lighting, shadow direction and texture stay perfectly consistent. The robot is the only thing on the planet with a fixed size, and must always be readable as the size reference in frame.

OPENING (0-3s): deep space. SCOUT drifts in orbit above a blue-grey world where the surface features refuse to settle — a crater rim swells to swallow half the planet, then shrinks to a speck, while the coastline of shattered plates stays exactly the same shape — visibly NOT Earth. A small golden holographic counter floats beside its helmet reading exactly "FRAGMENT 010 / 512". The robot dives.

THE DIVE (3-16s): mid-fall SCOUT transforms into its fighter-jet form and drops nose-first through air full of drifting stones that grow from grains to boulders as the jet nears them and dwindle again behind it. A sweeping continuous flyover: skimming a plain of pebbles that rise into a mountain range as the jet approaches, forcing it to climb, then flatten back to gravel underneath it; threading a canyon whose walls widen from a slot to a hundred kilometres of open sky in three seconds; then the landmark — the Anvil, a single blue-grey monolith that from every distance measures exactly the same in the frame, no matter how hard the jet flies at it: the one thing on this world that will not change size. Fast, fluid, majestic — no slow motion.

THE CHALLENGE (16-23s): the jet transforms back into SCOUT and runs at the Anvil — and gets no closer, the monolith holding its apparent size while the ground streams past. The robot tries the terrain instead: it leaps onto a pebble that becomes a cliff mid-leap, sprints its suddenly-enormous face, and jumps for a spire that shrinks to nothing under its boots, dropping it into a gulf that opens like a mouth. SCOUT fires its thrusters, catches the rim of a grain of sand now the size of a canyon wall, and hauls itself up — then runs BACKWARDS away from the Anvil, and the monolith finally rushes toward it.

THE FRAGMENT (23-28s): the Anvil's face splits open at exactly the robot's own height, and inside, on a thin column of light, rests the artifact from the reference image: a single fist-sized golden metallic cube etched with glowing circuit glyphs — and it is the only other thing here that stays one size, sitting perfectly still in SCOUT's palm. SCOUT takes it. The glyphs flare, and one breathtaking beat fires: across the whole planet every restless landform stops swelling and shrinking at once and locks into a single true size, mountains crashing to a halt as mountains, pebbles settling as pebbles, the horizon snapping straight for the first time.

THE RITUAL CLOSE (28-30s): close-up on SCOUT's open palm holding the ONE cube — there is only ever this single cube on screen. Its glyphs pulse and golden holographic text appears in the air beside it reading exactly "FRAGMENT — SECURED" (this exact text, two words only, NO numbers or digits anywhere in it). A calm, epic voice speaks the episode's only words: "Fragment ten... secured." Final frame holds on the ice-white robot and the single glowing cube.
