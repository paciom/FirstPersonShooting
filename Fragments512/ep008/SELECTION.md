# Episode 008 — BOLT — domain: STELLAR PLASMA

Counter: `FRAGMENT 008 / 512`. Spoken line: "Fragment eight... secured."

Every candidate leans on magnetism, arcs, filaments and charge rather than
molten rock, so none of them can be re-rolled as a lava planet. Bolt's own
electrical nature is the mechanism of the challenge in all ten — it charges,
grounds, degausses or flips polarity to survive.

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — the Arch Sea.** The surface of a dying star: a boiling white-gold
   plain sprouting tens of thousands of colossal crimson magnetic arches; Bolt
   runs down a collapsing arch and the cube waits in the cold black whirlpool at
   its foot.
2. **cand_02 — the Braid.** A world with no rock at all — a ball of woven plasma
   ropes turning around a caged star; Bolt runs the ropes magnetically as the
   weave unwinds, and the cube sits in the eye of the Crown Knot.
3. **cand_03 — the Curtain Fields.** A perpetual aurora-storm world of hanging
   charged curtains that only hold your weight while you hold a charge; Bolt's
   charge dies mid-leap and it falls through three sheets before recharging.
4. **cand_04 — the Fire Meridian.** A black world whose rivers of liquid light
   leave the ground at the equator and arc through the sky to the poles; Bolt
   rides inside the current until the planet's magnetism flips and it runs
   backwards.
5. **cand_05 — the Needle Steppe.** A magnetism so violent the iron ground stands
   up as billions of glowing needles that comb in waves; Bolt, being metal, is
   snapped rigid and pinned, and must dump its charge to crawl to the null point.
6. **cand_06 — the Flare Coast.** A world hugging a flaring star, its plain
   bristling with lightning-rod spires; Bolt makes itself the ground path and
   takes a flare strike through its own arms to keep climbing the Mast.
7. **cand_07 — the Static Flats.** Everything carries the same charge, so dust
   hovers in stacked floors and mountain-sized ball-lightning spheres drift
   between them; Bolt is shoved back until it flips polarity and gets snatched in.
8. **cand_08 — the Coronal Well.** A shaft of open magnetism bored a thousand
   kilometres down into a star, with a hurricane of charge howling up it; a
   vertical descent episode, cube in the cold black eye on the floor.
9. **cand_09 — the Petrified Storm.** A world where a million lightning bolts
   struck and never finished — solid forked columns of stopped electricity
   standing everywhere, re-firing in visible waves; Bolt times its steps to the
   wave, mistimes one, then deliberately becomes the circuit and rides it.
10. **cand_10 — the Quicklight Sea.** A shoreless ocean of liquid light whose
    waves break UPWARD into walls of arcs; Bolt runs across the surface and
    descends the spinning wall of the whirl that feeds the world's arc-pillar.

## Pick: cand_09 — the Petrified Storm

The central image explains itself in half a second with no narration: a plain of
lightning bolts that struck and simply stayed standing, casting long blue
shadows to the horizon — nothing about it can be mistaken for lava, ice or
anything Earthly, and it reads perfectly in a tall 9:16 frame because the
subject is vertical by nature. The challenge could not exist anywhere else:
crossing a field of stopped lightning by stepping only on the cold columns while
a re-fire wave sweeps in, then giving up on dodging and deliberately joining the
circuit to be thrown from column to column — a setback and a recovery that only
a robot made of charge could earn. The reaction beat is the strongest of the
ten: every stopped bolt on the planet fires at once in gold, one planet-wide
flash finishing a storm a million years old, which rhymes with the whole series'
promise of unfinished things being completed. Its landmark, the Strike — an
inverted mountain of lightning plunging into a crater where every bolt on the
world converges — gives the dive a clean escalating destination.
