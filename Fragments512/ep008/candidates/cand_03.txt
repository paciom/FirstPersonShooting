THE WORLD — the Curtain Fields: a world where the storm never ends and there is no ground to see. Instead there are CURTAINS: sheets of charged green, violet and crimson light hanging kilometres tall, rank behind rank to the horizon, rippling and folding like something enormous breathing. The curtains are solid — but only to things that carry a charge — so the folded crests of the great sheets are ridges you can run along, with nothing but black depth beneath.

CHARGE RULE: a charged body stands on the curtains; a neutral body falls straight through them as if they were not there. Bolt's lightning-bolt antennas burn blue while it holds a charge and dim as the charge drains over a few seconds. Touching any crimson fold recharges it instantly in a burst of sparks. Sound-free, weightless-looking, but everything obeys this one rule.
