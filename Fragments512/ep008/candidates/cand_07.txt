THE WORLD — the Static Flats: a world where the dust has not settled in a billion years. Ground and dust carry the same charge, so the dust hovers: level sheets of glittering ochre grit floating one above another like stacked floors, from the surface up to a kilometre high, each sheet dead flat and perfectly still. Between the floors drift the world's only weather — SPHERES of ball lightning, thousands of them, from fist-sized to mountain-sized, wandering slowly through the layers, humming, each one glowing from inside and never once touching another.

LIKE-CHARGE RULE: on this world nothing touches anything. Everything repels everything, which is why the dust floats and why the spheres slide past each other and never merge. A charged body can stand and run on the hovering dust sheets as if they were solid floors, but it is shoved away by every sphere it approaches. Dump the charge and you become neutral — and then you fall straight through every floor.
