# Episode 013 — candidate selection

Domain: SOLID LIGHT. Hero: TITAN (heavy blue-and-yellow armored robot, broad
yellow pauldrons, blue visor-slit head, red core gem). Counter "FRAGMENT 013 /
512"; voice line "Fragment thirteen... secured."

## The ten candidates

1. **cand_01 — THE GREAT LENS.** A whole planet that is one transparent optic,
   firing a focused white spear of sunlight into space; the robot shields
   itself with a shard of frozen glare to walk into the focal point.
2. **cand_02 — THE SUNLATTICE.** No ground at all: every sunbeam that reaches
   this place hardens into a solid glowing girder, and drifting dust-shoals
   delete the bridges wherever their shadow falls.
3. **cand_03 — THE POURED DARK.** Shadows have weight and pour off the crags
   as heavy black rivers into mirror-still lakes; the robot sinks in its own
   shadow and burns free with its chest gem.
4. **cand_04 — THE SEVENFOLD DAY.** Daylight lands already split into vast
   sweeping colour bands, each with its own physics: red crushes, blue lifts,
   violet springs — and all seven braid back into one white column.
5. **cand_05 — THE LINGER.** Everything leaves solid glowing copies of itself
   where it was; the robot climbs a spiral stair built from its own
   afterimages, racing the fade.
6. **cand_06 — THE HANGING DOUBLE.** Mirages are solid: an inverted duplicate
   landscape hangs above the real one, and it evaporates as the sun creeps.
7. **cand_07 — THE LOOM.** Light falls as long solid fibre that the wind
   braids into cables and a woven planetary canopy; the robot hand-braids a
   cable of light and swings the gap.
8. **cand_08 — PHOTONFALL.** Light falls as hard glassy needles that pile into
   drifts; shadow-hollows are the only shelter, and a slab of packed light
   makes a shield.
9. **cand_09 — THE FRINGE.** A mirror-crust world where down-light and
   up-light interfere into hundreds of floating hard terraces that slide as
   the sun creeps; the robot climbs by timing the rhythm.
10. **cand_10 — THE POUR.** Liquid light runs in incandescent rivers off black
    basalt into a kilometres-tall cataract; the cube waits in a dry shadow
    alcove behind the falling brightness.

## Pick: cand_02 — THE SUNLATTICE

It is the most other-worldly of the ten because it removes the planet
entirely: there is no ground, no sea and no horizon, only a radial scaffold of
frozen sunbeams hanging in a void with stars showing through the gaps — an
orbit shot that could never be mistaken for Earth. The strangeness is also the
most instantly legible: a heavy armored robot standing on a sunbeam explains
the entire world in a single frame, with zero narration, which matters more
than anything in a 30-second vertical cut. The challenge is impossible
anywhere else — a drifting dust-shoal's shadow erases the bridges one by one
while TITAN runs across them, giving a clean setback (a girder vanishes
mid-stride and it hangs from a beam-stump) and a clean recovery (it reads the
shoal's edge and times the gap) with no explanation needed. And the reaction
beat is the strongest in the batch: taking the cube makes light re-freeze
across the whole void, whole new spans and arches snapping into being to the
horizon and sealing the Shadow Scar — a world visibly rebuilt by the fragment,
which is exactly the series' promise of Earth's rebirth in one image.
