PRIVACY

Jet Armor Heroes · last updated 4 August 2026

The short version: no advertising, nothing is ever sold or shared, no third-party analytics or tracking tools of any kind, and every mode plays fine without an account. What little we collect exists to keep the game working, and it deletes itself.

Play data

The game stores one randomly generated player code on your device and sends us anonymous play events — which game mode was played, how long a match lasted, which robot was picked, whether the game hit an error, and how fast it loaded. We use this code and these events only to:

  • see which modes and robots get played, so we build more of what is fun;
  • find bugs and performance problems;
  • keep the service secure and working.

The player code is random: it cannot be used to contact you or your child, it is never combined with a name or an email address, and it is never used for advertising or profiling. Play events are deleted automatically after 90 days.

This website

jah.cc sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Page views are counted with a random number that changes on every visit, so visits cannot be tracked from one day to the next.

Accounts (optional)

Creating an account is never required. If you make one, we store the username you choose, a password, and an email address, so your name and stats can follow you between devices. While you are signed in, play events are linked to your account number — never to your username or email — so those stats work. Account details are used for signing in and for nothing else: no newsletters, no marketing, no sharing.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no ad identifiers are read or sent.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any data with anyone.
  • No third-party analytics, trackers, or social plugins.
  • No chat between players and no free-text the game publishes.
