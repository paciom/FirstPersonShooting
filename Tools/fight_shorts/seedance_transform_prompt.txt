A wide-open, eerie, beautiful natural arena — no city, no stadium, no crowd.

Opening frame, deliberately asymmetric and artistic: the orange panther robot looms as a dark out-of-focus silhouette filling the right foreground edge of the frame, while the cyan samurai robot stands small and razor-sharp in the middle distance at the lower left, its reflection perfect in the still water; the low sun flares between them.

Seconds 8-14: the cyan samurai robot transforms into its tank form and fires glowing explosive shells; the orange panther robot transforms into its fighter jet form, screaming between the shell bursts and answering with missile volleys and laser beams that carve steam trails across the water. Seconds 22-30: both transform back into robots in mid-air and collide, trading a final blur of fist strikes; the last impact sends a circular shockwave across the lake. Final shot: both robots land in low fighting stances among drifting spores and falling embers, their reflections rippling toward the camera.
