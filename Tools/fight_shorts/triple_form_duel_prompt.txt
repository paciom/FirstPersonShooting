A 30-second cinematic robot battle, fast-paced and relentlessly kinetic, like the best action sequence of a big-budget sci-fi film. Vertical 9:16 composition.

THE FIGHTERS. The cyan samurai robot with the horned helmet is the first reference image; its fighter-jet form is the second reference image and its tank form is the third. The orange panther robot with the dark navy visor is the fourth reference image; its fighter-jet form is the fifth and its tank form is the sixth. Both robots keep their exact colours and designs in every form: cyan and silver for the samurai, orange and dark navy for the panther, so the two are never confused.

TRANSFORM RULE — the most important rule: every transformation is a fast, clean, complete fold that finishes in under half a second. At any given instant each machine is FULLY a robot, FULLY a jet, or FULLY a tank — never a half-and-half hybrid, never a jet with legs, never a tank with arms. Each transformation reads as a sharp mechanical snap of folding panels, and the new form immediately does something: fires, flies, rams or strikes.

THE ARENA: a vast dry lake-bed of cracked black glass under a bruised violet storm sky, ringed by the shattered stumps of enormous alien towers. Wide open ground for tanks, open air above for jets, and broken tower walls to run up and leap from. Lightning flickers across the clouds; dust sheets blow across the glass; a low red sun sits on the horizon behind the ruins.

0-4s — THE CHARGE: the two robots stand facing each other across the black glass in fighting stances, cloaks of dust streaming past them. They sprint at each other and collide in mid-air, fists locked, and are thrown apart, skidding backwards on their heels.

4-9s — ROBOT MELEE: a fast, complex hand-to-hand exchange at full speed — the samurai throws three quick punches, the panther blocks all three with its forearms, spins and lands a hook; the samurai catches the arm, throws the panther over its shoulder into the glass; the panther rolls, sweeps the samurai's legs, and both flip back upright. Sparks burst off every block.

9-14s — TANK VERSUS JET: the samurai snaps into its TANK form, tracks biting the glass, and fires — a glowing shell that cracks the ground. The panther snaps into its JET form, screams low over the blast, and strafes the tank with cannon fire, sparks walking across its armour. The tank swivels its turret, tracking the jet across the storm sky, and fires again.

14-19s — DOGFIGHT: the samurai tank snaps into its JET form and launches after the panther. The two jets tear through the ruined towers nose-first, weaving between broken columns, trading cannon fire and missiles that burst against the stone. The panther jet flips into a hard turn; the samurai jet cuts inside it, and the two nearly clip wings.

19-24s — TANK RAM: both jets slam down onto the lake-bed and snap into TANK form mid-slide, throwing twin sheets of dust. They charge straight at each other, cannons firing point-blank, shells bursting off angled armour — and collide hull to hull in a shower of sparks, tracks screaming as each shoves the other backwards.

24-28s — THE FINISH: both tanks burst upward into ROBOT form mid-air and meet in a final flurry — block, block, spinning elbow, knee — and the samurai lands one clean strike that hurls the panther back across the glass in a long spray of dust.

28-30s: the panther catches itself on one hand and rises into a low stance; the samurai settles into its stance opposite. Neither is beaten. The camera holds on the two of them squared off as dust and sparks blow past, storm light flaring behind.

PACE RULE: everything runs at full speed with fast cuts, hard camera moves, whip pans and punch-ins. NO slow motion anywhere, no speed ramps, no floating.

Audio: a driving, hard-hitting orchestral and electronic battle score with heavy percussion that builds through the whole fight and hits its peak on the final strike, layered with metal impacts, servo snaps on each transformation, engine roars, cannon fire and wind. There is NO dialogue and NO singing and NO narration of any kind.

Photoreal detail, dramatic storm lighting, both machines always crisp and readable in frame, cinematic quality, unforgettable.
